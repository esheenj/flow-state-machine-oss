    Security and authority boundary

    This is a local, reversible display simulation only. A click is not an authenticated human decision; approved state is not authority to execute. The model rejects invalid replay, conflicting IDs and impossible transitions. It does not inspect, write, trade, transfer, read secrets, send analytics or open network connections.
