# Community conduct

Contributions should be respectful, specific and evidence-based. Discuss the code, tests and observable outcomes rather than personal traits. Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, threats, spam or publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Keep issues focused on reproducible open-source educational behavior. Maintainers may moderate, close or restrict disruptive discussions under GitHub's community tools. Use a private security channel for sensitive vulnerability details where enabled.
